Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Soapy helps you stay away from porn by blocking the words and websites you choose. That list is deeply personal, so Soapy is built to keep it on your phone and nowhere else.

The short version

  • Soapy has no accounts, no sign-up and no ads.
  • Soapy does not have internet permission. It cannot send your words, sites, PIN or anything else off your phone.
  • Your blocklist, PIN and security answer live only in Soapy's private storage on your device.
  • We do not see, collect, sell, share or analyse your data, because we never receive it.
  • The sections below explain each part in detail.

1. Who we are

Soapy is an Android app made by Peter Wisdom K. ("we", "us", "the Developer"). This policy explains what Soapy does with information when you use it. It applies to the Soapy app only.

2. What Soapy stores on your device

Everything below is saved inside Soapy's private app storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.

  • Your blocklist: every word and website you add, and the date you added it.
  • Your app lock, if you turn it on: a scrambled (salted, one-way hashed) version of your 4-digit PIN. The PIN itself is never stored.
  • Your security question and answer, if you set a lock. The question is stored as text so Soapy can show it to you. The answer is stored only as a salted, one-way hash.
  • Simple settings, such as whether the lock is on.

Soapy does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, files or any account.

3. Information Soapy does not collect

  • No personal identifiers, device identifiers or advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • No browsing history. Soapy does not keep a record of what you searched, which sites you visited, or when a block happened.
  • No usage statistics sent anywhere.

4. The Accessibility Service

To block a search or site, Soapy uses Android's Accessibility Service. You turn it on yourself in Android settings, and you can turn it off there at any time.

What it looks at: while you use a supported web browser, Soapy reads the text in the address bar and search box. It compares that text, on your phone, against your blocklist. It also watches the Android Settings, app-installer and Play Store screens, only to notice when you open the uninstall or deactivate screen for Soapy.

What it does: when a blocked word or site matches, Soapy sends you to the home screen and shows a message on top. When you open Soapy's uninstall screen, it shows the uninstall question.

What it does not do: it does not store the text it reads, does not log it, does not send it anywhere, and does not read passwords, messages, or any other app. The text is checked in memory and then discarded.

Soapy uses this service only for these purposes. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling or any other reason.

5. Device Administrator

If you turn on Uninstall protection, Soapy becomes a device administrator. This makes Android require you to deactivate Soapy first before it can be uninstalled, and lets Soapy show its question at that moment.

Soapy does not use this permission to lock, wipe, track or control your device or to read your data. You can deactivate it at any time in Android settings.

6. Screen overlays

The messages Soapy shows over other apps are drawn through the Accessibility Service. They are visual only. They do not capture your screen, record anything or read what is beneath them.

7. Permissions Soapy uses

  • Accessibility Service: detect blocked words and the uninstall screen, as described above.
  • Device Administrator (optional): make uninstalling take an extra step.

Soapy does not request internet, network, storage, location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone or SMS permissions.

8. Sharing your information

We do not share your information with anyone. We do not sell it. We have nothing to hand over to advertisers, data brokers, employers, family members, courts or anyone else, because we never receive your data.

9. Links to other apps and websites

In Settings, Soapy shows links to other apps by the same Developer (Discovr, Chatify and Resellr) on APKPure. Tapping one opens your browser or the APKPure app. From that moment you are on a third-party service, and their privacy policy applies, not this one. Soapy does not pass any information about you in those links.

10. How long we keep information, and deleting it

Your data stays on your phone until you remove it. Because the blocklist is meant to be permanent, Soapy has no button to delete individual words or sites.

You can erase everything Soapy holds by clearing Soapy's storage in Android (Settings > Apps > Soapy > Storage > Clear data) or by uninstalling Soapy. If Uninstall protection is on, deactivate it in Android settings first. Erasing the data also erases your blocklist and your lock.

Soapy turns off Android's automatic app backup, so your data is not copied to cloud backups.

11. Security

  • Your PIN and security answer are protected with salted PBKDF2 hashing, so they cannot be read back.
  • Data sits in app-private storage, which other apps cannot access on a normally working Android device.
  • Soapy cannot protect against a rooted or compromised device, or someone who knows your PIN and security answer. Choose a PIN and an answer that others cannot guess.

No system is perfectly secure. Keeping everything on your phone, with no internet access, removes the biggest risk: a server that could be breached.

12. Your rights

Under laws such as the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and similar laws elsewhere, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal data. Soapy gives you direct control: everything is visible to you in the app, and you can erase it all as described in section 10.

Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing on our side to look up or delete. If you have a question about your rights, contact us using the details below.

13. Children

Soapy is for adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Since Soapy collects no information at all, nothing is gathered from any user of any age.

14. If Soapy ever goes online

Future versions may add optional online features, for example fresh motivational messages. If that happens, we will update this policy before release and explain exactly what is involved. We have no plan to upload your blocklist, PIN or security answer. Anything that sends information off your phone will be optional and clearly disclosed.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Soapy changes. The date at the top shows the latest version, and the current policy is always available in Soapy under Settings > Legal. Material changes will be announced in the app.
